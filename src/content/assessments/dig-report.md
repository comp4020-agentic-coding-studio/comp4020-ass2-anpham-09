## The brief

By week 5 you have surveyed your dig site, read its stratigraphy, analysed its
inscriptions, and inventoried its tools. The Dig Report brings those four
investigations together into a single document.

Your report should include:

1. **The site:** what the project was, its language, its approximate size, its date range of activity
2. **Stratigraphy:** your commit-frequency timeline, with the phases and boundary events you identified in week 2
3. **Inscriptions:** your commit-message analysis from week 3, including the single most diagnostic message
4. **Tool inventory:** your dependency analysis from week 4, including any abandoned dependencies
5. **Hypothesis:** your first cause-of-death diagnosis, supported by evidence from at least two of the above

1500 words, plus the timeline visualisation (which does not count toward the
word limit).

## What you submit

A PDF or Markdown document. The timeline visualisation may be an image or a
text-based chart — whatever communicates the pattern most clearly.
