## The brief

The Exhibit is the final presentation of your semester's work. Your audience
has never seen your dig site; your job is to make them care about it.

Structure your exhibit in five parts:

1. **The site:** what the project was, who built it, when it was active
2. **The excavation:** your key findings — stratigraphy, inscriptions, architecture, documentation, oral history if applicable
3. **The diagnosis:** cause of death, supported by the full weight of your evidence across the semester
4. **The ethics:** should this project be revived? Your position, and your reasoning
5. **The restoration:** what you brought back in week 11, what you changed, what you left alone, and why

Present in 10 minutes. Submit a written companion of 2000 words alongside your
presentation materials.

## What you submit

The presentation (slides or equivalent) and the written companion (PDF or
Markdown). The restoration diff, as a branch or fork URL, is part of the
submission.
