## The brief

The Dig Report was a survey. The Forensic Analysis is the deep investigation.

Your report should include:

1. **Architecture:** the site plan from week 5 — the structure the team built and what it reveals about how they thought
2. **Documentation layer:** the audit from week 6 — what was documented, what was not, and what the gap means
3. **Oral history:** what you learned from contacting a former maintainer, or from secondary sources if contact was not possible or not appropriate
4. **Revised diagnosis:** your cause-of-death, now supported by evidence from weeks 2-8. If your hypothesis changed from the Dig Report, explain what changed it. If it did not, explain what new evidence confirmed it.

1500 words.

## What you submit

A PDF or Markdown document, citing specific commits, files, documentation
artefacts, and (if applicable) oral-history sources.
