## Before the dig

Prepare your exhibit. Structure:

1. **The site:** what the project was, who built it, when it was active
2. **The excavation:** your stratigraphic timeline, inscriptions analysis, tool inventory, architecture diagram, documentation audit
3. **The diagnosis:** cause of death, supported by evidence
4. **The ethics:** should it be revived? Your position and your reasoning
5. **The restoration:** what you brought back, what you changed, what you left alone

The presentation is 10 minutes. The written companion is 2000 words. Both are
due at the end of this week.

## In the session

Present your exhibit. The audience asks questions — about your methods, your
evidence, your ethical choices, and your restoration decisions.

The best exhibits make the audience care about a codebase they have never seen
and will never use.

## Afterwards

Submit the written companion alongside your presentation materials. This is the
final assessment.
