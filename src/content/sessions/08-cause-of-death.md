## Before the dig

Review your findings from weeks 2–7. Using the taxonomy from this week's
lecture, draft a cause-of-death diagnosis for your dig site:

- **Maintainer burnout** — the last human commits are bug fixes with increasingly terse messages
- **Ecosystem shift** — the project's niche was swallowed by a platform change or a dominant competitor
- **Hostile fork** — a fork attracted the community and the original lost its contributors
- **Funding loss** — the project depended on a sponsor who withdrew
- **Mass dependency failure** — a key upstream dependency broke or was abandoned
- **Mission accomplished** — the project does what it set out to do, and silence is success
- **Rewrite trap** — a v2 branch was started, never finished, and the energy to maintain v1 was gone

## In the session

Present your diagnosis. The class cross-examines: what evidence supports your
reading, and what alternative explanation fits the same evidence? A project
that looks like burnout from the commit messages might look like ecosystem
shift from the dependency graph.

## Afterwards

**The Forensic Analysis is due next week.** Your cause-of-death diagnosis is
the centrepiece. Revise it based on today's feedback.
