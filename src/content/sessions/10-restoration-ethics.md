## Before the dig

Read your dig site's licence. Then answer three questions:

1. **Does the licence permit forking?** Almost all open-source licences do. This question is easy.
2. **Did the author intend for someone to fork it?** Was the repo archived with a note? Was there a "looking for maintainers" issue? Or did the author simply stop?
3. **Should you fork it?** This is the hard question, and the licence does not answer it.

Read at least one perspective on the ethics of forking:

- The maintainer's prerogative position: archiving a repo is a statement
- The community continuation position: open source belongs to everyone
- The Ship of Theseus position: how much can you change before it is new?

## In the session

Structured debate. Half the class argues for reviving a specific dig site; the
other half argues against. Switch sides halfway through.

## Afterwards

Write a 200-word position statement: should your dig site be revived? Under
what conditions? What would you owe the original authors?
