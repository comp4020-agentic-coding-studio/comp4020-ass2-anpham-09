## Before the dig

Choose one specific feature or module of your dig site to restore. Not the
whole project — one piece. The choice itself is a finding: which part is worth
saving?

Criteria for choosing:

- **Small enough** to restore in one session (a function, a component, a CLI command)
- **Representative** of the project's purpose (not a test helper or a config file)
- **Broken for a diagnosable reason** (a missing dependency, a changed API, a deprecated language feature)

## In the session

Restore your chosen piece. The principle is minimum intervention: change only
what is necessary to make it work. Do not refactor, do not modernise, do not
add features. A conservator stabilises a painting; they do not repaint it.

Document every change you make, in order, with a one-sentence justification.

## Afterwards

Push your restoration as a branch or fork. The diff is part of your exhibit
submission. Write 200 words on what you changed, what you deliberately did not
change, and why the distinction matters.
