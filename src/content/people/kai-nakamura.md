Kai took over maintenance of two mid-sized Node.js libraries in 2024 after
their original authors stepped away — one by announcement, one by silence. The
experience of inheriting a codebase with no handover, no architecture document,
and twelve months of unanswered issues is what they bring to the course.

They run the weekly digs, supervise dig-site selection, and handle the ethics
review for oral-history interviews. Their office hours are Thursdays, or by
appointment after a particularly confusing `git blame`.

Kai maintains that the silence in an issue tracker is more informative than the
noise, and that most students spend week one reading code when they should be
reading dates.
