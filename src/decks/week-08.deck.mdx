# Cause of death

SLOP8374 · Week 8

---

## The taxonomy

- Maintainer burnout
- Ecosystem shift
- Hostile fork
- Funding loss
- Mass dependency failure
- Mission accomplished
- Rewrite trap

---

## Differential diagnosis

Burnout and ecosystem shift look identical in the commit graph: activity falls,
then stops.

The dependency manifest separates them. Burnout leaves the tool kit intact.
Ecosystem shift leaves it stranded.

---

## The rewrite trap

A v2 branch is started. It is more interesting than v1.

v1 stops receiving attention because it is about to be replaced. v2 is never
finished. Both are now dead.

This kills more projects than any external force.

---

## Case study: CoffeeScript

CoffeeScript won. Its best ideas — arrow functions, destructuring, classes,
template strings — went into ES6.

Then it had no reason to exist.

Success is a cause of death, and one that looks exactly like abandonment from
the outside.

---

## Misdiagnosis costs

Calling a project **dead** when it is dormant insults someone who is coming
back.

Calling it **done** when it is abandoned leaves users exposed.

Calling it **abandoned** when it is finished is just failing to read.

---

## Most deaths are plural

A dependency broke, which made the work unrewarding, while the niche was being
absorbed by a platform, during a year the maintainer had no time.

Pick a primary cause. Name the others. Do not pretend it was simple.
