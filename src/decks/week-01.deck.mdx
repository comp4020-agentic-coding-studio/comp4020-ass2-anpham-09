---

## Dead, dormant, or done?

Three kinds of silence:

- **Dead:** the maintainer left, the issues pile up, the dependencies rot
- **Dormant:** the maintainer will come back (they say)
- **Done:** the project does what it set out to do. Silence is success.

This course studies all three — but mostly the first.

## Case study: Google Reader

- Launched 2005. Killed 2013.
- Cause of death: corporate strategy — Google+ needed the users.
- Mourned by millions. Archived by nobody.
- The RSS reader ecosystem never recovered.

What would an archaeologist find in the ruins?

---

## Your dig site

Criteria:

- **Public** — you need to read the history
- **Abandoned** — no human commit in 18+ months
- **Licensed** — you will fork it in week 11
- **Rich enough** — at least 100 commits, at least 2 contributors
- **Dead enough** — not "on hiatus," not "looking for maintainers"
